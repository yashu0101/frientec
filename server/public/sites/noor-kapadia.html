Questions

Straight answers.

How many photographs do I get?

For a full wedding day, typically 600 to 900 edited frames. For a portrait sitting, 40 to 60. There is no separate "premium" set held back — everything worth keeping is in what you receive.

How long until I see them?

A preview set of about 40 images within 72 hours, so you have something to share while the day is still fresh. The complete gallery, graded by hand, lands within three weeks.

Do you travel?

Yes — anywhere in India, and abroad for destination weddings. Travel and stay are billed at cost with no markup. Anything within 150 km of Pune counts as local.

Do you still shoot film?

For portrait sittings and one roll on most wedding days, yes — medium format black and white, developed locally. Scans arrive with the digital set and there is no extra charge for it.

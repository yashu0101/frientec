Questions

Straight answers.

How are fees calculated?

Hourly for advisory and disputes, fixed fee for defined pieces of work such as an agreement, a notice or a title search. You get the estimate in writing before we start, and we tell you as soon as it looks like the estimate will be exceeded rather than at the end.

Will I deal with a partner or an associate?

A partner takes your instruction and a partner signs off everything that leaves the office. Associates do the work in between, which is what keeps the bill sensible. You will know who is doing what.

What happens to what I tell you?

Communications with us in a professional capacity are privileged under section 132 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023. We run a conflict check before taking instructions and will decline the matter if one exists.

Do you appear outside Maharashtra?

We appear before courts and tribunals across Maharashtra and instruct counsel elsewhere in India where a matter requires it, staying on the file as instructing advocates so you keep one point of contact.

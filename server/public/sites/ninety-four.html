The method

One cup, start to finish.

–02:00Dose

Weigh the beans

Fifteen grams for a 250 ml cup. That is a 1:16 ratio and it is the only number worth being fussy about — everything else on this page you can feel your way to.

15.0 g1:16Scale to 0.1 g
–01:30Grind

Grind medium, grind fresh

Coarse as table salt. Grind it right before you brew — coffee loses most of what makes it interesting within about fifteen minutes of being ground, and no amount of care later gets that back.

MediumBurr grinderGrind at brew time
–00:30Heat

Water at ninety-four

Off the boil, wait thirty seconds. Boiling water scorches a light roast and tastes hollow; too cool and it never gets going at all. Rinse the filter with the first of it and throw that water away.

94°CRinse the paperWarm the carafe
00:00Bloom

Wet it and wait

Forty-five grams of water, just enough to soak the bed. It swells and gives off carbon dioxide — that is the fizzing. Wait forty-five seconds. Skipping this is the single most common reason a cup tastes sour.

45 gWait 0:45Bed swells
00:45Pour

Three pours, slow spiral

Pour to 150 g, let it drop, then to 250 g. Spiral out from the middle and stop short of the paper — water running down the side of the filter never touches coffee and thins the cup.

To 250 g3 poursKeep the bed flat
03:30Serve

Drawdown, then drink it

The bed should run dry around three-thirty and sit flat, not craterous. Swirl the carafe once and pour. It is best in the first few minutes, which is the actual reason to make it by hand.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need a special kettle?

It helps and it is not essential. A gooseneck gives you a slow, aimed stream, which keeps the bed flat. Without one, pour more gently and from lower down, and accept that the first few cups will be uneven.

My coffee tastes sour. What did I do?

Usually under-extraction: grind finer, use water closer to 94°C, or slow the pour so the total runs nearer four minutes. If it tastes bitter and dry instead, do the opposite — coarser grind, quicker pour.

How should I store the beans?

In the bag they arrived in, sealed, at room temperature and out of sunlight. Not the fridge — coffee takes on the smell of everything around it and condensation ruins it. Whole bean, ground only as you brew.

Do you ship outside Pune?

Across India, dispatched within four days of the roast date, usually two to four days in transit. Orders placed before Tuesday go out on that week's roast.

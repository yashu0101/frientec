Questions

Straight answers.

How do you price a job?

Item rate against a measured bill for defined scopes, or a lump sum where the design is frozen. Either way you get the rate build-up — material, labour, plant and margin shown separately — so a variation later is arithmetic instead of an argument.

What happens if the programme slips?

You get told in the weekly report the week it happens, with the recovery plan attached. Delay damages sit in our contracts because we would rather carry that risk than manage a client who has stopped believing the dates.

Do you work on occupied buildings?

Yes — retrofit and façade replacement with the building running. That means night pours, dust and noise windows agreed with the residents in advance, and a separate site access that never crosses the occupied lobby.

Who is liable after handover?

Structural work carries a five-year defect liability, waterproofing three, and MEP one year from commissioning. Retention is released against a closed snag list, not against a date.
